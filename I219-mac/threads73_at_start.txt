  **Repeated `form` calls append the same crossing again.**

  `Walker::form` appends a crossing whenever `prev` brackets a sign change of `A0`. It does not consume `prev` or record that the crossing was reported. `advance` sets `prev`, and `set_y` clears it. Neither changes `prev` during `form`. A caller that calls `form` twice before `advance` therefore appends the same crossing twice. `WalkOut::crossings` then lists one double root more than once.

  Each accepted step must contribute at most one crossing. A repeated `form` call at the same point must not append a second entry for that step.
