Supplementary videos for
"Binary Ellis-Bronnikov wormholes in 3+1 numerical relativity: mergers,
fly-bys and gravitational waves", Nikita M. Shirokov and Ilya Nachevsky.

Twelve movies, 1920x1080 H.264, numbered as the paper cites them (Video 1-12).
Playback is real time (ten code units per second) except Video 2, whose frames
are two units apart and which plays at 2x, as stated on its frames. Every video
stops at its run's trust window. The same movies are on YouTube at the links
below, which the paper's captions also carry.

video01_wormhole_throat_collapses.mp4
  One throat, pure-quadrupole seed: it closes, and a horizon forms at t = 33.
  Paper: Fig. 4 (collapse branch).  https://youtu.be/Cc87mHcBPOA

video02_wormhole_throat_inflates.mp4
  The same throat, kicked inward: it keeps opening to t = 218, anti-trapped.
  Paper: Fig. 4 (inflation branch).  https://youtu.be/RIQCitu4tSc

video03_headon_collision_makes_black_hole.mp4
  Two throats fall together from rest at d = 8; a common horizon closes over
  the pair at t = 18.  Paper: Fig. 6.  https://youtu.be/3sx9GHRTYVs

video04_wormhole_flyby_no_merger_mouths_inflate.mp4
  d = 12, p = 0.25: a fly-by. No merger, no horizon, both mouths inflate.
  Paper: Sec. VII.  https://youtu.be/fh3pU7xCU_I

video05_plunge_merger_no_horizon_yet.mp4
  d = 12, p = 0.60: contact at t = 40, the mouths merge, and no trapped
  surface converges through t = 80.  Paper: Sec. VII.
  https://youtu.be/-ejFKpetL1Y

video06_hardest_plunge_mouths_inflate.mp4
  d = 12, p = 0.90: the hardest plunge; the mouths inflate on the approach.
  Paper: Sec. VII.  https://youtu.be/yzvwjC46lJY

video07_spiral_merger_makes_black_hole.mp4
  The off-axis merger (d = 6, p = 0.10) through its wall to the black hole
  it settles to.  Paper: Fig. 7.  https://youtu.be/jNnMAXPjyRA

video08_control_two_black_holes_collide_headon.mp4
  Vacuum control of the head-on: bare black holes at the same d, no exotic
  matter.  Paper: Fig. 8 caption.  https://youtu.be/aRWvD4W8OOg

video09_control_two_black_holes_fly_apart.mp4
  Vacuum control at p = 0.45: the holes coast apart.  Paper: Fig. 8 caption.
  https://youtu.be/65_3kw4obvE

video10_control_two_black_holes_merge.mp4
  The vacuum twin (d = 12, p = 0.12): plunge, merger, ringdown.
  Paper: Fig. 9 caption.  https://youtu.be/NkcpGiJIxXI

video11_headon_collision_far_zone_waves.mp4
  The head-on of Video 3 in a box four times wider, to t = 250, detectors to
  R = 180.  Paper: Fig. 8 caption.  https://youtu.be/vesOxaP5GSg

video12_wormholes_push_apart_mouths_inflate.mp4
  Two like-signed throats released from rest repel, and both mouths inflate.
  Paper: Sec. V C.  https://youtu.be/FnbDs-0n5zw
