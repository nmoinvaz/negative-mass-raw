### 3.2 The conversion

Substitution (DG06 (12) and DG08's own definitions, stated before use in `lemma1_conversion.py`): ζ = (2λr − 3μ) Ψ_z with μ = −2M, and 2λr − 3μ = 2λr + 6M = 2λ(r − r_s) since r_s = −3M/λ. With Ψ_z = c₋₁/ρ + c₀ + c₁ρ + …, ρ = r − r_s:

    ζ = 2λ(c₋₁ + c₀ρ + c₁ρ² + …),  ζ(r_s) = 2λc₋₁,  ζ′(r_s) = 2λc₀,
    ζ′(r_s)/ζ(r_s) = c₀/c₋₁ = λ²/(3M(2λ+3)) = −2λ²/(3μ(2λ+3))   (M = −μ/2).

This is c* of #146 (`zerilli_gauge_rs.md` §1): the sign is **minus** in μ, plus in M, and DG06 v2's printed (cz) is −1 times it. `lemma1_conversion.py` does the substitution first and compares with c* only in its last step (C1), and re-derives (crc) from DG08's own (RW2), (RW2a) (A3: K and H₁ pole-free iff c₀ = λ²c₋₁/(3M(3+2λ))).
