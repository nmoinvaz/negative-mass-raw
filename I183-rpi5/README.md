# I183-rpi5: the conical family off #167's grid, grid, start rule and criteria

Machine rpi5-ubuntu (Raspberry Pi 5, aarch64, 4 cores, 8 GB, shared with a choptuik-large-d runner). Issue
https://github.com/nmoinvaz/negative-mass/issues/183. Worktree `~/Source/negative-mass-i183`, branch
`wip/i183-conical-offgrid` (at main a3578f4). One process at a time, nice 10, `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=2`. Rust 1.100.0,
multishoot =4.2.0, multisolver =0.2.2, multidouble =5.1.0 (as #167, #184). Written before the first run of record; the
development runs that shaped it are `dev_00` to `dev_07` in this folder and are listed under "Amendment A1".
Everything numerical is **numerical evidence**; no enclosure is computed.

## The question

N = 0 (`det_classify.conical_N()`, cubic in F'') has at eta -> +infinity the two-parameter family
F = F_inf - 2 F_inf/eta^2 + (C + F_inf (F_inf^2 + 7) ln eta)/eta^4 + ... (#159). #167 integrated it inward on 187 points,
|F_inf| in [0.25, 4], |C| <= 3000, and found no regular crossing of eta = 0. This issue repeats the scan off that grid.
For every point: a regular crossing of eta = 0 matched to both series (then the family, and whether any is even), or the
first singular eta* with its kind, to a stated tolerance, with every error bar named.

## What is built

- `notes/warp/conical_offgrid.py` (a **new script**, not a mode of `conical_global.py`), its output
  `notes/warp/conical_offgrid_output.txt`, the note `notes/warp/conical_offgrid.md`.
- Crate `notes/warp/conical_global/`, additions only (the earlier binaries behave as before by default): `src/offgrid.rs`
  and `src/bin/offgrid.rs` (verdict, series start, walk, bisect, ntable), the locator in `src/hp.rs` behind
  `HpCfg::rstop` (None by default; `HpCfg::tiny`, default 1e-13 as before), `src/tables_hp.rs` (the series with every
  coefficient an exact sum of 8 f64 parts, generated by `conical_offgrid.py --gen-hp`), `--rstop` and two planted
  blow-ups (`pl6`, `pl7`) in `src/bin/loglog.rs`. The walker is #184's `hp_walk` (step variable s = (x - x0)/l with l a
  power of two, so the Taylor coefficients do not underflow at eta = 1e6), lane-precision constants, double-, quad- and
  octo-double; the scan runs in double-double (order 30, tol 1e-32, `conserve`: N constant), quad-double (60, 1e-64) is the
  cross-check.
- Harness fixes in the scripts this issue extends (`conical_global.py`, `conical_loglog.py`): every option except the
  harness's own is forwarded to the control subprocesses (`--raw167`, `--bin`, ...), `--dry-controls` prints the
  control command lines (test of the forwarding), and `--control-checks=all` runs every check under each control. The
  default there stays `targets` (only the target checks run, said plainly in their docstrings, printed in their output,
  in this README and in the note), because every check under every control takes hours. `conical_offgrid.py` runs every
  check under each control, on the `ctl` grid (below).
- multidouble's series module (`mullow`, `Relaxed`) is not used: #184 measured no gain below octo-double order 120
  (`Relaxed` forms no squares at double-double order 30 and quad-double order 60), and the walks here are double- and
  quad-double. No library change, no pull request.

## Start rule

The series (F, F', F'') of the family at eta = +L, the series of #167 through eta^-17 (36 terms with the logarithms), is
evaluated in the lane's precision at L = 200 4^k, k = 0 .. 6 (200, 800, 3200, 12800, 51200, 204800, 819200). The start is the
smallest L with the residual |N| / sum|terms of N| <= 1e-20 at (F, F', F'') and L. A point with no such L is `NOSTART`
and the acceptance fails for it (reported, not loosened). The walk then goes inward to eta = 1e-6.

## Verdicts

- `SINGULAR`: the locator (Domb-Sykes fit of the ratios of the Taylor coefficients, last ten orders, three fields; five
  consecutive real estimates ahead with R <= **1e-12 max(1, |x|)**, the pass of record) stops: eta* = x - R of the last
  estimate, **bar = the spread (max - min) of the last five estimates of eta*** (a spread over steps, not an enclosure).
  The **first pass** is R <= 1e-8 max(1, |x|) (as #167's record pass, absolute for x <= 1), also run, and the points where
  the two differ are listed. Kind labels as #167's `conical_kind` (`F->0`, `F->inf`, `a3->0`, `fold`, `other`). For `F->0`,
  alpha = 2/(eta*^2 + 4) is recorded against the fitted exponent.
- `FLOOR`: the walk reaches eta = 1e-6. `log` if |F'(1e-6)| > 2 |F'(1e-2)| (F' ~ p0/eta, p0 = eta F' recorded at 1e-2,
  1e-4, 1e-6), else `bounded`: continued to eta = -200 and fitted to the series there (`THROUGH`; global if F_inf^- equals
  F_inf to 1e-10 and the mismatch is <= 1e-10).
- `NOSTART`, `STEPLIMIT`, `NONFINITE`, `TINYSTEP`: failures of the point, reported (`TINYSTEP`: a step fell below
  **1e-15 max(|x|, 1e-6)**, the guard). Each `TINYSTEP` point is read from its last steps: `shrinking` if h and the median
  Taylor radius of F'' fall by a factor >= 1.05 at each of the last five steps (the walk closes on a singularity, a log-type
  end at the kappa curve), else `stalled` (stalls at the guard); the last steps are printed.

## Acceptance criteria (each can fail; coded as worded here)

| id | check | criterion |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | `n_table` | the lane's N (`hp.rs`, double-double) at 40 rational points equals `det_classify.conical_N()` and `conical_limit.build_N()`, worst \|dN\|/sum\|terms\| <= 1e-26 |
| O2 | `series_hp` | `tables_hp.rs` equals the regenerated table; the lane series at 6 pairs (F_inf, C, k): (0.001, 1e5, 6), (0.01, -1e6, 5), (0.1, 3e7, 6), (1, 1e8, 6), (16, 3e5, 2), (128, -1e4, 3) equals the mpmath series to 1e-26 (double-double, 50 digits), 1e-55 (quad-double, 90 digits) relative; the chosen start residual of each is <= 1e-20 |
| O3 | `plant_167` | #167's planted matching (linear, cubic) and blow-ups (cube, sqrt, both directions) through the same crate code: errors of C-, e, C+ <= 1e-20, nodes <= 1e-22, blow-up eta* error and bar <= 1e-8 and kinds right |
| O4 | `plant_far` | the planted blow-ups F'' = 2F^3 (x* = 0.7321) and F F'' + F'^2 = 0 started at x0 = 1e6 and walked inward by `hp_walk` with the locator: x* error <= 1e-8, bar <= 1e-8 (double- and quad-double); the planted linear equation (1 + x^2)F'' + 4xF' + 2(F - 7/10) = 0, exact alpha + (a + b x)/(1 + x^2), from 1e6 to 1: relative error of F, F', F'' at 1e3, 10, 1 <= 1e-24 (double-double), 1e-50 (quad-double) |
| O5 | `regress167` | #167's 187 points through `offgrid` and through #167's `conical_global scan --rstop=1e-8` (both run here, the same absolute R <= 1e-8): class and kind equal, eta* within 1e-8 where both bars are <= 1e-9 |
| O6 | `scan` | every grid point has a verdict: none is `NOSTART`, `STEPLIMIT`, `NONFINITE` or `TINYSTEP` (**a `TINYSTEP` point makes this NOT MET**); start residual <= 1e-20; every `SINGULAR` has bar <= 1e-8 max(1, eta*); the number of `THROUGH` points is printed (a regular crossing would be reported with its family parameters, evenness and the series fit at -200) |
| O7 | `spread` | the same point from the next start radius (4 L, if <= 819200 and its residual <= 1e-20): same class and kind, eta* within 1e-8 max(1, eta*), `FLOOR` p0 (at 1e-6) within a relative 1e-9; the number of points with no second start is printed |
| O8 | `dd_qd` | quad-double against double-double on every point with L >= 204800 plus the 30 `SINGULAR` points of smallest eta* and the 30 of largest: same class and kind, eta* within 1e-12 max(1, eta*), `FLOOR` p0 within a relative 1e-12 |
| O9 | `modes` | `project` (Newton on F'' each step) against `conserve` (N constant) over the full grid: class and kind equal, eta* within 1e-10 max(1, eta*), p0 within 1e-10 |
| O10 | `symmetry` | (F_inf, C) -> (-F_inf, -C) over the full grid: class and kind equal, eta* within 1e-10 max(1, eta*), \|p0\| within a relative 1e-10 |
| O11 | `n4_factor` | sympy: the lowest-degree part of N (degree 4 in F, F', F'') is -(eta F'' + F')^2 [(eta^2+1)(eta^2+2) F'^2 + 2F((eta^2+1)F'' + eta F')]; and with F'/F = 1/(2 chi), chi = sqrt(eta^2+1) h, h = K + 7/8 asinh(eta) + eta sqrt(eta^2+1)/8, the second factor vanishes identically (a Bernoulli equation for psi = 1/chi) |
| O12 | `closed_form` | the F_inf -> 0 limit of O11: c = C/F_inf = 8K + 7 ln 2 + 19/4, F -> 0 at the root eta* of h for c < 7 ln 2 + 19/4. The crate's eta* at F_inf = 0.04, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005 for c in {-1e4, -1e3, -100, -10, 0, 3, 6} against the root of h (mpmath, 40 digits, bisection): relative difference <= 1e-4 at F_inf = 0.005, and the difference falls by a factor 4 +- 1 at each halving of F_inf (O(F_inf^2)); the relation c(K) is the one matched to the series at infinity by hand |
| O13 | `bisect` | every adjacent pair of different class label on the lines is bisected to relative width 1e-12; the two ends keep different labels, and no end is `THROUGH`, `NOSTART`, `STEPLIMIT`, `NONFINITE` or `TINYSTEP`; both ends printed with eta*, kind, p0 |
| O14 | `independent` | three off-grid points (0.1, 3e4), (5, 3000), (16, -1e4) (chosen before any verdict) from the series at the chosen L to eta = 20, integrated with mpmath (`odefun`, 25 digits, tol 1e-15, root of the cubic nearest the previous F''): relative F, F' agree with the crate to 1e-9; shares N and the series with the crate |
| O15 | `recip_check` | a quad-double `conserve` walk of (0.1, 3e4) to eta = 20 with every step halved (`--hscale 0.5`) changes F, F' by <= 1e-55 relative; with the constants rounded to double-double (`--mutate=recip-dd`) it must exceed 1e-45. (`project` gives 1e-41 here at the first steps, `dev_04`: the series start's N residual enters through the projection; `conserve` gives 1e-59.) |
| O16 | `step_halving` | 12 points: order 30 / 1e-32 against order 36 / 1e-38 (double-double): same class and kind, eta* within 1e-10 max(1, eta*), p0 within 1e-10 |

integration has -6 -> -5.9999 on F F'^2 F'' eta^2), `regress-kind` (#167's kind compared as the wrong string),

## Harness fixes (#184's review, on #183)

1. `conical_loglog.py` forwarded no `--raw167` (and `conical_global.py` no `--bin`) to the control subprocesses; a control
   outside the standard layout crashed or read as silent. Fixed in both: every option except the harness's own

   every check under each control in both old scripts (default `targets`, stated plainly in the docstrings and in the
   output: `controls run: the target checks only`). `conical_offgrid.py` always runs every check.

## Stop conditions and logs

A run not finished in 1 h of wall time is stopped (own systemd unit only). The run of record is two units: the checks on the
full grid, and the controls (`--controls-only`, each control a subprocess on the `ctl` grid), whose logs together are
`conical_offgrid_output.txt`. No condition depends on memory. Every log is kept, including failed and stopped runs, and a
rerun is numbered (`run_02`).
